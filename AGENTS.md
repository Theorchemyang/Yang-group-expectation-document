# Editing Instructions

This repository contains the Yang Group Expectation Document.

When editing the document:

- Preserve Yang's direct, personal, and supportive voice.
- Use clear professional English without making the document sound overly legalistic or corporate.
- Maintain the existing heading and numbering structure.
- Preserve firm expectations when they reflect important group principles.
- Emphasize integrity, kindness, responsibility, scientific rigor, and mutual respect.
- Distinguish group expectations from formal UW–Madison employment policies.
- Avoid unnecessary repetition.
- Do not modify unrelated sections unless explicitly requested.
- Show substantive changes clearly for review.
